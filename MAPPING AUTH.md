The Template Approach
You're right — no login for most of these. Here's the thinking:

App Type	Auth Required?	Why
Invoice Maker	Optional	Save drafts across devices
Link-in-Bio	Required	You need to own a username
Post Generator	Required	OAuth tokens per platform
Natural Reader	None	Paste text, pick voice, play. Done.
Scanner	None	Camera → PDF. Local only.
Meditation	None	Pick session, press play.
Podcast Maker	Optional	Save generated episodes
The template should default to no auth with an optional "Sign in to sync across devices" tucked into settings. Much lower friction.
